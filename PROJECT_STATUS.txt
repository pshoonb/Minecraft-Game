RPG Core Prototype 0.2.7

Target:
- Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.1
- NeoForge 21.1.265
- Java 21
- ModDevGradle 2.0.148

Implemented in source:
- RPG level mirrors the vanilla player experience level.
- Vanilla level changes synchronize to the RPG attribute screen.
- Attribute points are based on the highest vanilla level reached.
- Eight assignable attributes: Strength, Defense, Health, Mining Speed, Movement Speed, Jump Height, Luck, Experience Loot.
- Free attribute reset.
- Server-authoritative attribute allocation and reset.
- Per-player attachment persistence and UUID-checked client sync.
- Attribute screen displays the eight intended attributes and their allocated points.
- Intended effects are documented in the UI; seven gameplay conversions are marked as not implemented until balance rules are set.
- Experience Loot effect text is calculated from the same provisional balance constant as the server XP calculation.
- Experience Loot participation tracking refreshes on successful player damage and expires after 60 seconds.
- Offline participants remain eligible for 60 seconds after their last successful hit and use their most recent logout snapshot.
- Each LivingExperienceDropEvent batch uses the agreed per-orb denomination calculation.
- Nearby pre-existing XP orbs are not searched or rewritten; multi-batch XP drops are processed separately.

Not implemented:
- Numeric Minecraft gameplay conversion for Strength, Defense, Health, Mining Speed, Movement Speed, Jump Height and Luck; their point allocation and intended effects are present, but the actual rates/effects are not connected.
- Final balancing of Experience Loot's provisional +2% per point.
- Exploration, quest, gathering, monster scaling, equipment and loot systems.

Data note:
- The project does not yet have players using these attributes. The previous placeholder fields Agility, Constitution, Intelligence and Spirit have been replaced by the eight intended fields without a player-data migration.

Validation:
- Source and documentation changes are committed to main.
- A fresh Gradle/NeoForge build and in-game test are required for this revision.
