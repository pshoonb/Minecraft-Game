RPG Core Prototype 0.3.0

Target:
- Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.1
- NeoForge 21.1.265
- Java 21

Implemented:
- RPG level mirrors the vanilla player experience level.
- Attribute points are based on the highest vanilla level ever reached, so spending vanilla levels does not revoke earned points.
- Five assignable attributes: Strength, Agility, Constitution, Intelligence, Spirit.
- Agreed cumulative attribute-point formula.
- Free attribute reset.
- Server-authoritative attribute allocation and reset.
- Per-player attachment persistence and UUID-checked client sync.
- P-key attribute screen with English and Simplified Chinese localization.
- Removed diagnostic counter UI/network payload and separate RPG XP progression.
- First-pass gameplay effects for current RPG level, Agility, and Constitution.
- Effects use stable transient attribute modifiers, recalculated on level changes, allocation, reset, login, and respawn.

Prototype tuning values (subject to playtesting):
- Level: +0.50 max health and +0.05 armor per square-root level.
- Agility: +0.15% movement speed per square-root attribute point.
- Constitution: +0.50 max health and +0.08 armor per square-root attribute point.
- Per-attribute point count is capped at 10,000 for effect calculations; square-root scaling prevents linear runaway.
- Strength, Intelligence, and Spirit are stored/allocatable but do not yet affect gameplay.

Not implemented:
- Final balancing of the first-pass values.
- Gameplay roles for Strength, Intelligence, and Spirit; no skills or class system.
- Exploration, quests, gathering-specific rewards, monster scaling, equipment, or loot systems.
- Full multiplayer and compatibility testing with other attribute-modifying mods.

Validation required:
- GitHub Actions build and in-game testing for this revision.
- Verify health, armor, movement speed, respec, level spending, login/respawn persistence, and multiplayer isolation.
