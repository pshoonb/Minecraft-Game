RPG Core Prototype 0.3.0

Target:
- Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.1
- NeoForge 21.1.265
- Java 21

Implemented:
- Seven agreed attributes and the unchanged level-point formula.
- Level 100-150 is the primary balance target.
- A logarithmic soft-cap curve: first 25 effective points are linear; later points continue to add diminishing returns without a hard cap.
- Same attribute modifiers on the authoritative server and owning client.
- Jump velocity adjustment on local prediction and server.
- Defense adds armor, final damage reduction after vanilla armor/enchantment handling, reduced burning time, and explosion knockback resistance.
- Mining Speed affects both general block-break speed and suitable-tool mining efficiency.
- Per-player persistent data, server-authoritative allocation, UUID-checked sync, and free reset remain.

Current tuned values per effective attribute point:
- Strength: +0.10 attack damage.
- Health: +0.25 maximum health.
- Defense: +0.15 armor (direct armor bonus capped at +20), plus up to 40% additional final damage reduction, 60% burning-time reduction, and 70% explosion knockback resistance.
- Mining Speed: +1% block-break speed and +0.15 mining efficiency.
- Luck: +0.10 vanilla Luck.
- Speed: +0.5% movement speed.
- Jump Height: +0.5% upward jump velocity.

Progression reference:
- Level 100: 7,550 total attribute points.
- Level 150: 15,675 total attribute points.
- When evenly divided among seven attributes, effective points are approximately 89 at level 100 and 100 at level 150.
- The soft cap continues to grow beyond the practical level range; there is no 10,000-point hard cap.

Notes:
- Vanilla Luck only affects loot tables/mechanics that read the player's Luck value.
- Actual mining depends on tool/block suitability; Jump Height changes launch velocity and jump height is nonlinear.
- The agreed level reward formula is unchanged.

Legacy migration:
- Strength allocation is retained.
- Removed prototype attribute allocations are refunded.
- Current level, highest level, and total earned points remain.

Not implemented:
- Final in-game balance validation and full compatibility testing.

Validation required:
- Test all seven effects in-game, including ordinary/fire/explosion damage.
- Test reset, level spending, login/respawn persistence, and multiplayer isolation.
