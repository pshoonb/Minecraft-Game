RPG Core Prototype 0.3.0

Target:
- Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.1
- NeoForge 21.1.265
- Java 21

Implemented:
- Seven agreed attributes and the unchanged level-point formula.
- Level 100-150 is the primary balance target.
- Logarithmic soft-cap curve: first 25 effective points are linear; later points continue to add diminishing returns without a hard cap.
- Attribute modifiers are applied on the authoritative server and owning client.
- Jump Height also increases safe fall distance and reduces fall damage.
- Defense adds armor, final damage reduction after vanilla armor/enchantment handling, reduced burning time, and explosion knockback resistance.
- Mining Speed affects both general block-break speed and suitable-tool mining efficiency.
- Luck adds extra resource drops for vanilla ore blocks, using the server-side BlockDropsEvent; vanilla Fortune results are duplicated as extra rolls, but silk-touch ore-block drops are not copied.
- Attribute GUI has +1, +10, and +100 allocation buttons; each button spends min(requested, available).
- Per-player persistent data, server-authoritative allocation, UUID-checked sync, and free reset remain.

Current tuning per effective attribute point:
- Strength: +0.20 attack damage.
- Health: +0.35 maximum health.
- Defense: +0.30 armor (direct armor capped at +20), plus up to 40% additional final damage reduction, 60% burning-time reduction, and 70% explosion knockback resistance.
- Mining Speed: +2% block-break speed and +0.30 mining efficiency.
- Luck: +0.20 vanilla Luck; one extra ore-resource roll per 40 effective Luck, with fractional progress as chance.
- Speed: +1.2% movement speed.
- Jump Height: +1.5% upward jump velocity, +0.75 safe-fall distance per effective point, and up to 75% additional fall damage reduction.

Notes:
- Luck mining bonus applies to coal, diamond, emerald, lapis, redstone, copper, iron, gold, nether quartz, and ancient debris drops; it does not duplicate ore blocks dropped by Silk Touch.
- Extra ore drops duplicate vanilla-calculated resource stacks, so Fortune results are preserved.
- Jump and fall-safety effects use the same saved Jump Height allocation.
- The agreed level reward formula is unchanged.

Legacy migration:
- Strength allocation is retained.
- Removed prototype attribute allocations are refunded.
- Current level, highest level, and total earned points remain.

Not implemented:
- Final in-game balancing and full compatibility testing.

Validation required:
- Test early allocations (10/25/50) for all attributes.
- Test mining Luck on several ore types, including Silk Touch behavior.
- Test Jump Height and fall safety together.
- Test +1/+10/+100, reset, level spending, login/respawn persistence, and multiplayer isolation.
