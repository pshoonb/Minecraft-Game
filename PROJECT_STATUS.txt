RPG Core Prototype 0.3.0

Target:
- Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.1
- NeoForge 21.1.265
- Java 21

Implemented:
- RPG level mirrors vanilla player experience level.
- Attribute points use the agreed cumulative formula and are preserved when vanilla levels are spent.
- Exactly seven attributes: Strength, Health, Defense, Mining Speed, Luck, Speed, and Jump Height.
- Free reset, server-authoritative allocation, per-player persistence, UUID-checked sync.
- P-key attribute GUI with Simplified Chinese and English.
- True soft-cap curve: first 25 points are linear; later points use logarithmic diminishing returns without a hard point cap.
- The same uniquely identified attribute modifiers are installed on server and owning client.
- Jump Height adjusts local client prediction and server-side player jump velocity.
- Defense applies a final damage reduction after vanilla armor/enchantment reductions, including damage types that bypass armor.
- Defense also reduces burning duration and explosion knockback.
- Mining Speed affects both overall block-break speed and mining efficiency when using a suitable tool.

Effect tuning per effective point:
- Strength: +0.75 attack damage.
- Health: +1.0 maximum health.
- Defense: +0.60 armor (modifier capped at +30), plus a soft-capped all-damage reduction approaching 65%, burning-time reduction approaching 60%, and explosion knockback resistance approaching 70%.
- Mining Speed: +5% block-break speed and +0.75 mining efficiency.
- Luck: +0.50 vanilla Luck attribute.
- Speed: +3% movement speed.
- Jump Height: +2.5% upward jump velocity.

Soft-cap function:
- E(p) = p when p <= 25.
- E(p) = 25 + 15 * ln(1 + (p - 25) / 15) when p > 25.
- There is no 10,000-point hard cap; late investment continues to add diminishing returns.

Notes:
- Vanilla Luck only affects loot tables/mechanics that actually read the player's Luck value.
- Defense's extra final-damage reduction is applied after the vanilla armor/enchantment stage.
- The agreed level reward formula is unchanged.

Legacy data upgrade:
- Previously allocated Strength is retained.
- Removed prototype allocations (Agility, Constitution, Intelligence, Spirit) are refunded.
- Current level, historical highest level, and total earned points remain.

Not implemented:
- Final balancing after in-game playtesting.
- Exploration, quests, gathering rewards, monster scaling, equipment, or loot systems.
- Full compatibility testing with other gameplay mods and multiplayer stress testing.

Validation required:
- GitHub Actions build and in-game tests for all seven effects, reset, level spending, login/respawn persistence, and multiplayer isolation.
