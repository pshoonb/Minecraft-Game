RPG Core Prototype 0.2.7

Target:
- Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.1
- NeoForge 21.1.265
- Java 21
- ModDevGradle 2.0.148

Implemented in source:
- RPG level mirrors the vanilla player experience level.
- Vanilla level changes synchronize to the RPG attribute screen.
- Attribute points are based on the highest vanilla level reached.
- Eight assignable attributes: Strength, Defense, Health, Mining Speed, Movement Speed, Jump Height, Luck, Experience Loot.
- Free attribute reset.
- Server-authoritative attribute allocation and reset.
- Per-player attachment persistence and UUID-checked client sync.
- Attribute screen fills the game window with a responsive two-column layout; it displays all eight attributes, their allocated points, and calculated effects. The inventory-screen button is the only entry point; the legacy keyboard mapping has been removed.
- Strength adds the cumulative 2/x result as flat Minecraft damage points to player-caused damage.
- Defense reduces all damage received by players with an asymptotic 80% soft cap.
- Health increases maximum health and heals by the same actual amount when maximum health rises.
- Mining Speed multiplies player block-breaking speed by its cumulative formula percentage.
- Movement Speed uses a transient multiplicative-base movement-speed modifier.
- Jump Height adds launch velocity corresponding to the cumulative height formula.
- Luck adds 1-15 extra items to eligible ore-block drops. Base weights are 150, 145, ... 80; the weight exponent flattens with current Luck so larger quantities become relatively more likely.
- Luck multiplies player-caused living-entity loot random-chance and random-chance-with-enchanted-bonus conditions by its cumulative percentage formula. Each eligible living-entity loot pool with at least one configured base roll also has at most one additional weighted roll, independently granted with chance min(100%, LuckBonusPercent). The additional roll uses the original pool entries and weights, preserving their relative proportions. Deterministic entries and conditions remain active on their normal roll and can be selected again by the additional roll.
- Worn-equipment drop chances are multiplied by the same cumulative Luck percentage formula.
- Experience Loot reuses the Mining Speed cumulative percentage formula.
- Each LivingExperienceDropEvent batch uses the agreed per-orb denomination calculation:
  T' = floor(T * (1 + bonusPercent / 100))
  Q = T' // (max(2, N) - 1)
  Each of the N logical orbs receives Q; integer division remainders are discarded.
- Experience Loot participation tracking refreshes on successful player damage and expires after 60 seconds.
- Offline participants remain eligible for 60 seconds after their last successful hit and use their most recent logout snapshot.
- Each LivingExperienceDropEvent batch uses the kill-time Experience Loot snapshot.
- Nearby pre-existing XP orbs are not searched or rewritten; multi-batch XP drops are processed separately.

Limitations / not yet implemented:
- Luck's loot-pool and deterministic-entry coverage is scoped to player-caused living-entity loot contexts. Pools with zero configured base rolls do not receive the extra roll. The extra roll does not alter pool conditions, entry weights, item counts, or loot functions.
- No dedicated in-game GUI test has been run; verify the full-window layout at different GUI scales and confirm the inventory button is the only way to open the screen.
- Exploration, quest, gathering, monster scaling, equipment and loot systems remain out of scope.

Implementation notes:
- Cumulative sums use exact evaluation for the first 8,192 points and short midpoint intervals thereafter to avoid unbounded work for high-level characters.
- Defense's 80% soft cap uses 80 * (1 - exp(-rawReduction / 80)).
- Player modifiers are transient and reapplied on login and respawn from persistent RPG attachment data.

User-provided marginal attribute formulas (x is the current number of points allocated to that attribute):
- Strength: f(x) = 2 / x; cumulative result is flat Minecraft damage points.
- Defense: f(x) = min(3, 20 / x) percentage points; total damage reduction uses an 80% soft cap.
- Health: f(x) = max(0.01, 0.5 / ln(x + e - 1)).
- Mining Speed: f(x) = 5 / ln(0.1*x + e - 0.1), as a percentage.
- Movement Speed: same marginal formula as Mining Speed, applied as a percentage.
- Jump Height: f(x) = 1 / ln(0.5*x + e - 0.5), as additional jump height.
- Luck: mineral extra-drop amounts are 1-15, using dynamic descending weights based on the base vector [150, 145, 140, 135, 130, 125, 120, 115, 110, 105, 100, 95, 90, 85, 80]. Higher Luck flattens the distribution, relatively favoring larger quantities.
- Luck's creature equipment-drop and living-entity loot probability bonuses reuse the Mining Speed cumulative percentage formula.
- Experience Loot reuses the Mining Speed cumulative percentage formula.

Luck probability implementation detail:
- For player-caused living-entity loot contexts (including player-owned projectiles), Luck applies the same cumulative Mining Speed percentage bonus to vanilla random-chance predicates and random-chance-with-enchanted-bonus predicates.
- The chance adjustment is mathematically equivalent to multiplying the predicate's existing probability by (1 + LuckBonusPercent / 100), capped by the predicate's natural 100% maximum. The enchanted-bonus predicate is adjusted after vanilla's Looting-based chance is calculated, so Looting and RPG Luck remain independent.
- Each eligible living-entity LootPool receives at most one extra vanilla weighted-selection roll, with probability min(100%, LuckBonusPercent), when its configured base roll count is positive. No entry weight or relative weight is rewritten; deterministic entries still execute normally and may be selected again during the additional roll.
